# Product guide

EventGenie supports weddings, birthdays, and community celebrations. Start from the landing page, choose an event type, and enter requirements through the four-step setup wizard. Samples are clearly labelled and work without AI configuration.

## Create and manage a plan

Review event details, guest count, currency, budget, style, food preferences, cultural preferences, and spending priorities before generation. An AI plan streams validated previews and saves only after the complete result passes validation. If generation fails or is cancelled, the wizard preserves your details.

The dashboard brings together the overview, allocations, preparation timeline, vendor checklist, ideas, invitations, warnings, and save status. Use **My plans** to reopen or delete individual plans. Storage is specific to the current browser and device.

## Decision Studio

| Tool                 | Workflow                                                                                                                                     |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| What-If Simulator    | Adjust budget, guests, priorities, contingency, or known costs. Inspect the comparison, save an alternative, and confirm before applying it. |
| Budget Rescue        | Review quote pressure and protected costs, compare calculated recovery options, and optionally request qualitative AI ideas.                 |
| Vendor Decision Desk | Enter supplier options and actual quotes, record booking and availability, and keep optional contact details locally.                        |
| Readiness            | Review overdue tasks, dependencies, compressed preparation, budget shortfalls, and missing supplier decisions.                               |
| Day-of Schedule      | Choose a same-day window and local time zone, load a sample or AI proposal, edit activities and links, and validate before saving.           |
| Targeted refinement  | Review proposed ideas, decor, invitation, inclusion, rainy-day, or children's schedule improvements alongside local original content.        |
| Export               | Print saved details, download an all-day calendar, or create and restore a selected-plan JSON backup.                                        |

**Load fictional planning examples** adds clearly labelled sample vendor/financial/schedule data to an eligible plan. These examples are not verified businesses or prices.

## Advanced planning

Select **Open advanced planning** from a saved plan to access:

- **Your next best steps:** prioritised unfinished tasks, readiness links, manual task editing, and optional reviewed task suggestions.
- **Compare strategies:** the current plan and up to three saved alternatives, with budget, reserve, protected costs, feasibility, and readiness context.
- **Contingency planning:** hypothetical rain, supplier cancellation, budget reduction, cost increase, additional guests, lower attendance, or a delayed task. Select supported changes and confirm one undoable revision.
- **Ask EventGenie:** plan-specific explanations grounded in relevant context. Exact financial answers are calculated locally. Up to five explicitly saved answers remain attached to the event.
- **Invitation Studio:** five tones, short/detailed variants, local templates, manual editing, and optional generic AI prose. Recorded event details are inserted by application code. Copy, text download, and optional native sharing remain user-initiated.

## Budgets and saved changes

INR is the default currency; USD, EUR, GBP, and JPY are also supported. Decimal precision follows the selected currency. Changing currency does not convert amounts.

Allocations are planning envelopes. Manual quotes, booked commitments, and paid expenses are separate records. Recorded payments do not transfer money. Applying a scenario does not cancel a booking, refund a deposit, or automatically scale supplier prices with attendance.

Saved edits support undo/redo, with 20 history entries by default. New edits clear redo. Stale tabs must reload the latest revision before overwriting newer data. Failed saves expose an unsaved state and retry controls; keep the tab open until saving succeeds.

## Practical limits

- AI recommendations require human review of cultural details, permissions, accessibility, dietary arrangements, timing, and supplier terms.
- Supplier categories do not identify verified local businesses or guarantee availability.
- Schedules support same-day events, not overnight windows. Calendar export uses all-day dates and does not infer time-zone offsets from invitation text.
- No accounts, automatic cross-device sync, guest/RSVP management, payment processing, invitation delivery, or public plan sharing are included.
- Clearing browser data can remove saved plans. Protect downloaded backups because they may contain private notes or vendor contacts.
